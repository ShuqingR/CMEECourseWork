  • Correctly targets lag-1 dependence (successive-year relationship) by correlating adjacent-year pairs ✅
  • Uses a permutation null that destroys temporal order, appropriate for testing whether adjacent-year correlation exceeds chance under exchangeability assumptions ✅
  • Reports both the observed statistic and permutation-based p-value ✅

Conceptual verdict: Clear understanding demonstrated.

(works, but a preallocated numeric vector would be clearer and slightly more efficient) ⚠
